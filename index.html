Pick your topic

Sit at the table for the topic you care most about. Your pick tints the site, chooses your three case studies, and is remembered on this device.

SIXTY MINUTES

The agenda,
and where you are in it

    DISCUSSION GROUND RULESHow we talk today

      STEP ONE · 10 MINUTES

      Your table,
      your starting point

      Introduce yourself. Then, before anyone reads a case, name what you are curious about and what worries you about your table’s topic.

      Your role at the table

      YOUR RESPONSE

      Quick debrief · 5 minutes

      Each table shares one curiosity and one concern with the room. Notetakers: catch what other tables say. You will want it for the principles step.

      STEP TWO · 15 MINUTES

      Three cases,
      side by side with your response

      STEP THREE · 15 MINUTES

      The longer debrief,
      across all three tables

      Tables report out. Listen for what repeats across course design, assessment, and feedback, and where the topics pull in different directions.

      YOUR RESPONSE

      STEP FOUR · 5 MINUTES · INDIVIDUAL

      Guiding principles
      you would actually follow

      Brainstorm values for your personal practice or a departmental policy around this issue. Write your own, or start from a seed and rewrite it in your words.
