# The Ethics of Using AI as Instructors

A faculty workshop on AI in course design, assessment, and grading/feedback, as a static, no-build website that doubles as each attendee’s worksheet and take-home.

**Created by Marc Watkins, Emily Donahue, and Danielle Clevenger · AI Institute & CETL, University of Mississippi.** Live at https://mwatkins03-netizen.github.io/Ethical-AI-In-Course-Design-Workshop-Fable-5.1/

- **Landing page**: looped ambient video above four illustrated step cards (SVG art made for this workshop in the *Teaching for Discernment* style), drawn with that site’s WebGL card renderer (hover lift, tilt, paper-bend shaders). Drag, Shift+scroll, or arrow-key through them; opening one wipes to that step.
- **Before we start**: pick your topic (course design / assessments / grading & feedback) and how you will respond: Talk it through (analog, print a case as a discussion sheet), Type it here, or Record my voice. Every response pane has its own Talk / Type / Record switch.
- **Step 1 · Curiosities and Concerns**: introductions plus curiosities and concerns about instructors’ use of AI.
- **Step 2 · Case Studies**: three verbatim cases per topic on the left, the four questions to consider on the right, plus a debrief note.
- **Step 3 · Takeaways, Questions, and Commitments**: your duty of care, a values/principles builder (personal practice or department/university policy), and the questions you leave with.
- **Step 4 · Take it with you**: export as a self-contained HTML file with recordings embedded, print-to-PDF, or plain text.
- **Stored on your device only**: header chip, hero badge, callouts, recorder notes, export header. No server, accounts, or analytics; localStorage + IndexedDB only.
- **Recording**: MediaRecorder audio saved on-device; optional live transcription into the editable text box (browser Web Speech API; needs HTTPS or localhost).
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA target for ADA Title II; keyboard-operable cards and panes, motion toggle, AA contrast, labeled fields, live-region status; accessibility statement under About. axe-core: 0 violations.
- **Brand**: University of Mississippi palette from the UM Brand Portal (Lyceum #CF142B, Oxford #142142, Magnolia, Powder Blue #006BA6, Faulkner #F8EDD9 paper, Tupelo/Landshark accents) and brand typefaces IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Serif via Google Fonts (Matrole, Handelson One, Termina are licensed and not embedded).

All content lives in `js/data.js` (case studies, instructions, questions, and prompts verbatim from the planning document; no attendee counts are published). No dependencies; deploy the folder to GitHub Pages as-is (`.nojekyll` included). See `AI-DISCLOSURE.md`.
